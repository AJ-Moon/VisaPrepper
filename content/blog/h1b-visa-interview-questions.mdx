H1B interviews are less about your personal story and more about your job: your employer, your role, and whether your qualifications genuinely match a specialty occupation. That makes them different from F1 or B1/B2 interviews, and it means the most common weak point is applicants who understand their work but struggle to explain it clearly out loud.

## What an H1B interview is actually checking

Officers reviewing an H1B application are generally trying to confirm that your role qualifies as a specialty occupation, that your qualifications genuinely match that role, and that your answers are consistent with the petition your employer filed on your behalf (Form I-129 and the underlying Labor Condition Application).

## Common H1B interview topics

### Your role and day-to-day duties

Practice questions:

- What is your job title, and what do you do day to day?
- Can you walk me through a typical week in your role?
- Who do you report to, and who reports to you, if anyone?

The goal is a clear, specific description that matches the duties listed in your petition — not a title alone.

### Your qualifications

Practice questions:

- What is your educational background, and how does it relate to this role?
- Do you have a specialized degree or equivalent experience required for this position?
- Why does this role require someone with your specific background?

If your degree field doesn't obviously match your job title, be ready to explain the connection clearly — this is a common follow-up point.

### Your employer and worksite

Practice questions:

- What does your employer do?
- Where will you be working, and is that different from your employer's main office?
- How long has the company been in business?

### Petition consistency

Practice questions:

- What is your annual salary?
- When does your employment start, or when did it start?
- Has anything about your role changed since your petition was filed?

Your answers here should match what's in your I-129 petition and LCA. See our [U.S. visa interview documents checklist](/blog/us-visa-interview-documents-checklist) for the H1B-specific documents worth reviewing beforehand.

### Employment history

Practice questions:

- What did you do before this role?
- If you changed employers recently, why did you make that move?
- Have you held H1B status before?

## Sample H1B interview questions to practice with

1. What is your job title and what are your main responsibilities?
2. How does your educational background qualify you for this role?
3. What does your employer's company do?
4. Where will you be working?
5. What is your salary, and when does your employment begin?
6. Why did this employer choose to sponsor you specifically?

## Mistakes that weaken H1B interview answers

- **Reciting a job title without explaining the actual work.** "I'm a software engineer" isn't enough on its own — be ready to describe what that means day to day.
- **A weak link between degree and duties.** If your qualifications and your role seem disconnected, address that directly instead of hoping it doesn't come up.
- **Not knowing basic facts about your employer.** You don't need financial details, but you should know what the company does and your place in it.
- **Inconsistency with the petition.** Salary, job title, or worksite that doesn't match your I-129 is worth resolving with your employer before your interview, not during it.
