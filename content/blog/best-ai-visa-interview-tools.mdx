Some services focus on spoken rehearsal; others also organize documents or compare application facts. Start by deciding what you need to practice, then check the format, feedback, allowance and access for your own case.

## What to try before choosing a tool

### 1. Use a detail from your actual application

Choose a non-sensitive fact you understand, such as the purpose of a trip or why a course fits your background. See whether the setup lets you supply relevant context. Do not upload private documents until you understand the service's privacy, deletion and retention practices.

### 2. Test whether a follow-up connects to your answer

An answer like “My father will sponsor me” leaves an obvious preparation task: explain the genuine funding arrangement and the costs it covers. A useful follow-up should help expose the missing detail. A more difficult tone or an animated officer alone does not establish that the practice is personalized.

### 3. Check what the feedback actually tells you to do

Look for a connection between your words, an issue that needs clarification and a practical next step. Review the transcript for recognition errors before accepting a finding. An AI score or simulated verdict is feedback about a rehearsal, not evidence of likely approval.

### 4. Understand what “document checking” includes

Ask whether the service provides a checklist, extracts fields, compares records, or compares speech with application information. Check supported formats, upload limits and what happens to revised bundles. Those distinctions matter more than a broad “AI document” label.

The State Department describes how the [DS-160 and interview are used together](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/forms/ds-160-online-nonimmigrant-visa-application.html). For preparation, it is sensible to understand the information you submitted before practicing an explanation. A third-party check does not authenticate a document or decide your eligibility.

### 5. Compare the whole allowance

Count sessions, minutes, checks, validity and renewal terms separately. One free minute, a short interview trial and a free document check solve different problems. Confirm whether the language you want is available for your route, rather than assuming a translated website means a translated interview.
