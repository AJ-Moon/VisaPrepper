Preparing for a U.S. visa interview in Pakistan? Start with three things you should be able to explain: **why you are going, who will pay, and what your plans are afterward**. You do not need a speech. You need to understand your own application.

This guide gives you questions to practice and small tasks to try at home. The questions are examples, not a list supplied by an embassy.

## Visitor visa interview questions: family visits and tourism

For a B1/B2 visit, practice answering:

1. Why do you want to visit the United States?
2. Who are you visiting, and how are you related?
3. How long will you stay, and where?
4. Who will pay for your flight and daily costs?
5. What work, studies, or responsibilities will you return to in Pakistan?

**Try this:** if you are visiting your sister, write down the occasion, intended length of stay, where you will stay, and how costs are shared. Then explain it aloud without reading.

If you run a shop or work in a family business, describe your actual work. “I manage stock and customer orders in our family shop” gives a clearer picture than “I do business.” Use it only if it describes your real role.

A relative's invitation is not a visa guarantee. The [Department of State's visitor guidance](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visitor.html) says an invitation letter is not required to apply for a visitor visa. Your own circumstances still matter.

## F1 student interview questions: explain your funding

For study, prepare these questions:

- Why this university and this course?
- How does it connect to your previous studies or work?
- Who will cover tuition, living costs, and travel?
- What does your sponsor do, and what funding is actually available?
- What are your plans after completing the course?

Make a private funding note using your own documents. Keep amounts in **Pakistani rupees (PKR)** and **U.S. dollars (USD)** clearly labelled. Do not mix a monthly income with a yearly course cost.

If a parent and a scholarship will both help, separate the two sources. Check what the scholarship covers and what remains for the family to pay. Do not describe hoped-for support as confirmed funding.

## If someone else filled in your DS-160

Ask to review the answers, not just the confirmation page. Check your job, travel purpose, U.S. contact, previous travel, and who is paying. If an answer is wrong or your circumstances have changed, follow official correction instructions; do not repeat an error just to sound consistent.

Our [DS-160 review guide](/blog/ds-160-interview-consistency) walks through the details worth checking.

## Work and family interviews need different preparation

For work, explain your real duties, employer, and work location. Start with the [H1B question guide](/blog/h1b-visa-interview-questions).

If you are moving through a family-based immigrant case, do not use a visitor checklist as your complete guide. Review the relationship and case details alongside your official instructions. Our [family visa preparation page](/visa-types/family-immigrant) explains the practice topics.

## Before travelling for your appointment

Whether you are travelling from Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, or another city, use the **location on your appointment confirmation**. Do not assume every Pakistani applicant attends the same post.

Check the date, entry instructions, documents, and travel arrangements before leaving. Use the [U.S. Mission Pakistan visa page](https://pk.usembassy.gov/visas/) to reach official services and notices. This article does not show live appointment slots, fees, or operational updates.

Start organizing with our [interview document checklist](/blog/us-visa-interview-documents-checklist), then follow the official list for your case.

## A short English or Urdu practice session

Choose one question. Explain the answer in the language you find easiest, then practice again in the language you intend to use. Ask someone to point out details they did not understand—not to write a new story for you. Confirm interview-language arrangements through official appointment channels.
