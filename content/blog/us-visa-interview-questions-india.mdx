India sends more F1 students and more H1B applicants to the United States than any other country, which means a huge amount of visa interview advice online is written with Indian applicants in mind — but much of it is still generic. This guide focuses on what's actually useful to prepare for, organized by the visa categories most relevant to Indian applicants.

## Where interviews happen

Nonimmigrant visa interviews for Indian applicants are conducted at the U.S. Embassy in New Delhi and U.S. consulates in Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, and Kolkata. Your appointment confirmation will specify exactly where you've been scheduled — it's worth double-checking this along with current appointment wait times, since they can vary significantly by location and season.

## F1 student visas from India

India is one of the largest source countries for F1 students, and interview volume at Indian posts during peak season (typically ahead of the U.S. fall semester) is high — which often means officers move through interviews quickly. Being able to explain your program, funding, and plans clearly and concisely matters more than a long, detailed narrative.

Common areas worth strengthening:

- Why you chose your specific university and program, beyond rankings
- A clear explanation of your funding — family support, education loans, assistantships, or a combination
- How your intended field of study connects to your academic background

See our full [F1 visa interview guide](/blog/f1-visa-interview-questions-and-answers) for the complete set of topics and practice questions.

## H1B work visas from India

Because India has been the largest source country for H1B visas for years, Indian applicants — especially in IT services and technology roles — should expect officers to be quite familiar with common role types, which can mean sharper follow-up questions about your specific position rather than generic ones.

Focus especially on:

- Clearly explaining your actual day-to-day responsibilities, not just your job title
- How your specific degree and experience match your role's requirements
- Your employer's business and your work location, especially if you'll be placed at a client site

See our full [H1B visa interview guide](/blog/h1b-visa-interview-questions) for practice questions and common mistakes.

## B1/B2 visitor visas from India

Business travel and family visits are both common among Indian B1/B2 applicants — from IT professionals traveling for client meetings to families visiting relatives across the large Indian diaspora in the U.S.

Focus especially on:

- A specific, believable purpose and itinerary for your trip
- Clear ties to home — your job, business, or family responsibilities in India
- For business travel, a clear explanation of your company and the purpose of your specific meetings

See our full [B1/B2 visa interview guide](/blog/b1-b2-visa-interview-questions) for more detail.

## Family-based and H4 applicants

With such a large number of H1B holders, H4 dependent applications are also common — see our [H4 visa interview guide](/blog/h4-visa-interview-questions) if you're applying as a spouse or child of an H1B holder. Family-based immigrant cases, reflecting long-settled Indian-American communities, are covered on our [family and immigrant visa page](/visa-types/family-immigrant).

## Preparing in Hindi — or your own language

Interviews are conducted in English, but many applicants think through their answers in Hindi or another Indian language first, which is completely normal. What matters most is a clear, confident explanation — not native-level phrasing.
