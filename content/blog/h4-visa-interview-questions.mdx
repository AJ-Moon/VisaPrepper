H4 interviews put many applicants in an unfamiliar position: the questions are often as much about your spouse's job as they are about you. That can feel disorienting if you've mostly prepared around your own background. This guide covers what H4 interviews typically focus on and how to prepare without over-relying on someone else's answers.

## What an H4 interview is actually checking

H4 status is for the spouse and unqualified children of an H1B visa holder (the "principal applicant"). Officers are generally confirming that your relationship to the principal applicant is genuine and properly documented, that you understand the basic shape of their status, and — if you're applying together — that your answers and your spouse's answers are consistent with each other.

## Common H4 interview topics

### Your relationship

Practice questions:

- How did you meet your spouse?
- When and where did you get married?
- Do you have children together, and are they applying with you?

Know your own relationship history well — dates, key milestones, and how the relationship developed — described naturally rather than rehearsed.

### Your principal applicant's status

Practice questions:

- What does your spouse do for work?
- Who is their employer?
- How long have they held H1B status?

You're not expected to know every detail of their job, but basic familiarity — their employer's name, general industry, and role — matters.

### Your plans in the United States

Practice questions:

- What will you do while your spouse is working?
- Do you plan to study, if you're eligible to?
- Who will care for any children, and what are the arrangements?

### Consistency between spouses

If you and your spouse are both interviewing (sometimes on the same day, sometimes separately), your answers about your relationship, your living situation, and your plans should naturally align — not because you rehearsed identical answers, but because they describe the same true situation.

## Sample H4 interview questions to practice with

1. How and when did you meet your spouse?
2. When did you get married, and where?
3. What does your spouse do, and for which company?
4. Do you have children? Are they applying with you?
5. What will you be doing while in the United States?
6. Has your spouse held H1B status before, and for how long?

## Mistakes that weaken H4 interview answers

- **Not knowing basic facts about the principal applicant's job.** A vague "I don't really know what he does" can raise questions, even in a genuine relationship.
- **Inconsistent relationship timeline.** Dates and key details about your relationship history should be consistent with what's in your application.
- **Treating it like a solo interview.** Unlike F1 or B1/B2, your case is directly tied to another person's status — prepare with that connection in mind.
